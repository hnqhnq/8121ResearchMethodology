## Abstract Writing

**Due: Fri Oct 23, 2026 11:59pm**



### Objective

The purpose of this assignment is to develop your ability to condense a substantial piece of research into a concise, self-contained abstract that meets the standards of a peer-reviewed journal. You will use your own Master's thesis as the source material.

### Task

Write an abstract of your Master's thesis, approximately 250–350 words, that could stand alone as the abstract of a publishable journal article. 

### Formatting

Length: 250–350 words

Font: Times New Roman, 12 pt, double-spaced

Include a title at the beginning 

Include 4–6 keywords at the end
